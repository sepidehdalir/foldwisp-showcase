Foldwisp privacy policy · effective date October 19, 2026

Foldwisp is a booklet-printing app by Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir. The app does not collect your documents or send them to a developer-operated server. It has no account, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK, external AI or app-operated cloud sync.

PDFs and printer notes

You choose PDFs through Files. A Files provider may download a document from its own cloud service. Foldwisp copies selected PDFs into protected local app storage and processes them on your device. Saved jobs and printer presets are excluded from device backup. They remain until you remove the job or preset, or delete the app. Keep your originals or save exports somewhere you control.

Export files are temporary app-cache files and are cleared at the next app launch. Files you explicitly save or share outside the app remain at your selected destination. Sharing and printing use the provider, recipient or printer you choose; their handling, settings and privacy policies apply.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and provides verified entitlement information through StoreKit. Foldwisp checks that entitlement on your device. We do not receive your payment details or operate a purchase-validation server. Apple's services operate under Apple's privacy policy.

Support

If you email hello@arvinify.com, we use the information you choose to send to answer your request. Please avoid sending private PDFs or payment information. You can contact that address about your support correspondence or request its deletion, subject to any records we must retain.

This website

This support site has no added analytics, forms or advertising. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub handles website requests under its privacy statement. Website hosting is separate from the app's on-device PDF processing.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date if the app's privacy practices change. Contact Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir at hello@arvinify.com or visit arvinify.com.
