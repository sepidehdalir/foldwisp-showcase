Make a first booklet

  1. Choose an unencrypted PDF, or try the original eight-page field guide.
  2. Select A4 or US Letter. Check Sheets and Fold & read.
  3. Use the two-sheet calibration guide before printing the full stack. Record which face, leading edge and stack order work with your printer.
  4. Export a PDF, save it to Files, share it or use system printing.

Free and Lifetime Pro

Complete booklets through eight source pages include PDF export for free. Lifetime Pro is a one-time App Store purchase for booklets up to 120 pages, eight reusable printer presets and batches of up to five separate PDFs. The app displays your localized price. Restore Purchases is in Pro and Settings.

Printing looks upside down or out of order

Printer feed paths vary. Start with the numbered two-sheet guide. Keep the paper size, printer tray and scaling settings consistent; note the face, leading edge and stack order. Test reverse-back-stack or 180° back rotation as needed. Confirm that the folded pages read 1–8 upright before using the saved directions. Foldwisp does not detect your printer's feed direction or provide drivers.

A PDF will not import

Use an unencrypted, flattened PDF up to 30 MB and 120 source pages, within the app's image-resource limits. Print annotations and forms need a flattened print copy. Nonprinting links are accepted but output is print-only. Source attachments, interactivity and digital signatures are not preserved.

Where are my files?

Saved jobs and presets are local to this app and excluded from backup. Keep original PDFs and important exports outside the app. Removing a job deletes its local copy; deleting the app removes its library. Sharing and printing send data only to destinations you select.
